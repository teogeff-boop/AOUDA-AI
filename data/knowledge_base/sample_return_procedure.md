# Sample Return and Container Sealing Procedure

## Container Preparation
Step 1: Unclip the sterile sample container from the utility belt. Verify that the inner seal O-ring is intact and clean of dust.

## Sample Collection
Step 2: Use the geological scoop to collect 200 grams of regolith or rock fragments. Carefully deposit into the tube without touching the inner walls.

## Sealing and Storage
Step 3: Rotate the container cap clockwise 180 degrees until a distinct click is heard. Verify the pressure seal indicator turns green.

## Logging and Transport
Step 4: Attach the RFID sample tag, record the current GPS position with AOUDA, and place the sealed container into the primary EVA sample pouch.
